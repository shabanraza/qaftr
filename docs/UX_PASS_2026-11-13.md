# UX pass — 2026-11-13

Scope: the five journeys in the brief (home → tools hub → free tax invoice → download → sign-up; document tools; checker tools; UAE; pricing + mobile menu), Arabic (RTL, default) and English, at 375 px (mobile, touch) and 1280 px. Benchmarks: `docs/COMPETITOR_UX_SPEC_2026-11-09.md` (Wafeq, Qoyod, Daftra, Zoho Books KSA, ClearTax/Tally calculators, Fatoora-style free tools).

Method: Playwright screenshots in Edge with the real web fonts, cut into segments, covering every journey page at both widths before and after the fixes. The in-app browser covered the mobile drawer and the hero. The screenshots stayed in the session scratchpad and aren't committed; the before/after table describes what changed.

## Fixes

### Round 1: conversion-critical issues and shared tokens (`styles.css`)
- **#1** The Download PDF and WhatsApp buttons keep their 40 px height on phones: `w-full sm:flex-1` replaces `flex-1` in `InstantInvoiceTool`.
- **#2** A new token, `--qaftr-gold-ink: #8a6420` (about 5.4:1 on white), is used for gold text on light surfaces: the document totals, the UAE generator and VAT calculator totals, the Saudi VAT calculator, the wave-checker countdown, the QR-decoder warnings, the 404 numeral and the tools-hub kicker. `--qaftr-gold` stays for fills, badges and text on dark green, where it passes.
- **#3** One rule makes outline, ghost and link buttons rendered as `<a>` brand green, darkening to ink on hover. The teal came from the unlayered global `a` rule, which beats Tailwind's layered `text-primary`. This fixes the hub cards, "حوّل إلى فاتورة ضريبية" and the `/ae` secondary CTA in one place.
- **#10** `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the entrance animations, transitions and hover lifts.
- **#15** RTL marketing pages reset `letter-spacing` to normal, so joined Arabic letters stay joined. English pages are unaffected.
- Native `<select>` elements (the UAE VAT category) use the same radius and white fill as the inputs.
- New assertions in `src/lib/marketing/button-contrast.test.ts` cover these rules.

### Round 2: forms and pricing
- **#4** Each pricing card ends with a full-width CTA. Free: "أصدر فاتورتك الأولى مجاناً", which scrolls to the tool. Pro: "إنشاء حساب / Create account", which opens the existing sign-up dialog. Both reuse existing strings and carry `data-cta="pricing_free|pricing_pro"` for the existing `cta_click` listener, with no new event names. The cards are bottom-aligned.
- **#5** Free invoice line items have visible labels (description, qty, unit price), and the placeholders that only repeated the label are gone. The existing `aria-label`s stay, so accessible names and tests don't change.
- **#6** Supply date and notes now sit in an "تفاصيل إضافية / Additional details" card (one new copy key, `detailsTitle`). Section card titles went from 14 px green to 16 px ink in the display font, so they read as headings above the field labels.
- **#7** The document tools and the UAE generator mark their form card with `.qaftr-form-sections`, which puts a hairline and 24 px of space above every section after the first. The rule sits on the `<legend>`, because a fieldset's border is drawn behind its legend. The qty/price grids use `items-end`, so a wrapped label no longer pushes its input out of line.
- **#9** Checklist items show a small dot while pending and a check when done, instead of radio-style empty circles.

### Round 3: hub and home polish
- **#8** The whole tool card on `/tools` is now clickable: the existing "Open tool" link is stretched over the card, so each card still has one link. Hover shows a subtle green border, and keyboard focus shows a ring. The badges come from `src/lib/tools/copy.ts`, which other agents are editing right now, so I left them alone (see remaining ideas).
- **#11** The "How it works" cards are start-aligned at every width.
- **#12** The hero mock shows a QR glyph instead of the letters "QR".

### Tried and reverted
- A non-breaking space to keep "ZATCA Phase 1" on one line in the English H1. `copy-positioning.test.ts` checks the exact H1 string, so the copy stays as it was.

## Remaining ideas (not done)
- Footer Tools/Guides column (#14). It would duplicate the tool registry, which the parallel XML-validator and wave-reminder branches are changing. Add it after those merge.
- Tools hub badges: show "New" only on the newest one or two tools, in `src/lib/tools/copy.ts`, once those branches have merged.
- The Arabic free plan card repeats itself ("مجاني" label above the "مجاناً" price). This is a copy fix in `i18n.ts`.
- "ZATCA Phase 1" can break across lines in the English hero H1. Either render it with a non-breaking space in the component, or update `copy-positioning.test.ts` together with the copy.
- TRN checker header duplication (#13). This is a copy decision, left to the copy owner.
- Hero product mock with a "connected to FATOORA" card (Qoyod style), once Phase 2 integration exists. Today that would be an inaccurate claim.
- Monthly/yearly pricing toggle ("save X%") and a VAT-exclusive footnote. The footnote needs a regulatory/copy review.
- Shorter mobile home: the six feature cards stack to about 1,500 px. A 2-column compact grid or a horizontal scroller would cut that.
- Native date inputs show `mm/dd/yyyy` on Arabic pages. A localised date picker would need a dependency.
- Merge the free invoice tool (`InstantInvoiceTool`, with its own card-per-section form) into `FormKit`/`DocumentShell`, so every generator shares one form system.
- UAE: per the 2026-11-13 scope change, `/ae` stays free tools only. This pass adds no UAE sign-up or account CTAs (the pricing CTAs are on the Saudi home only).
